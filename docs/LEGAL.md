# Legal use and what this repository holds

This project lets people who own Xenoblade Chronicles 2 for Nintendo Switch play their copy on an Android device. It
is unofficial and not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or Monolith Soft.

## What is here

- A script that drives nx2android on your computer (`setup.sh`).
- Documentation and two screenshots taken on the author's device.

## What is never here

- The game: its program, data, shaders, text, art, music or icon.
- Encryption keys, firmware, or instructions for getting them or for bypassing protections.
- A built APK or data folder. Both are made from your own files on your own computer and should stay with you: the APK
  carries the game's icon, and the data folder is the game.
- Links to copies of the game, keys or firmware.
- Fan translations.

## Your part

- Use only a copy of the game and keys that you obtained lawfully, from a console you own.
- Keep what `setup.sh` builds to yourself.
- Do not post game files, keys, or links to them in issues or discussions. Such posts will be removed.

## What the port knows about the game

The 60 fps patch is tied to one build of the game (update 2.1.0): it names addresses in the game's program. That
description lives in nx2android's game profile, not here, and contains no game code.
